The metrics

Every metric is taken on the drawing as shown, in the tool's own coordinates: clones are nodes, nodes a tool leaves out are absent, routed edges are the polylines drawn. General graph-drawing metrics come from gdMetriX; the pathway-map metrics and the comparison with the reference are this package's.
