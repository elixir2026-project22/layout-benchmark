Summary of scores

Per layout and metric: its median rank over the models (1 = the best of that model's layouts; ties share the mean rank), with the number of models ranked in brackets. A layout is ranked on a model only where it has a value: not where it failed, nor where a metric doesn't apply (overlaps without node sizes, compartments without boxes, similarity without a reference). Every metric is taken on the drawing as shown, in the tool's own coordinates (see the README). There is no combined score yet: the weights of the metrics are still to be decided. Click a column to sort; hover a header for what it measures. The header's top row says where each metric comes from; the metrics below says what each measures and how. The best median rank per column is highlighted.

The metrics

Every metric is taken on the drawing as shown, in the tool's own coordinates: clones are nodes, nodes a tool leaves out are absent, routed edges are the polylines drawn. General graph-drawing metrics come from gdMetriX; the pathway-map metrics follow the layout rules of BioHackathon Europe 2026 project 22 (Open Pathway Maps), as its pathway layout scorer and sbml_pathway_layout define them, computed by this benchmark (raw values, no thresholds), next to a few checks of this benchmark's own; the comparison with the reference is this benchmark's.
